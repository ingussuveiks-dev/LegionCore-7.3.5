# Highlord Kruul's Twisted Reflection

Highlord Kruul `117198` now casts the native `234676` at the challenger during the final step of scenario `1259`. Its two-second cast uses native interrupts and reflect. An actual incoming damage event on the cursed challenger can cause Kruul to heal through native spell `234678` for 5% of the recipient's maximum health. When the cast is reflected and the debuff lands on Kruul, damage from the challenger or their controlled unit heals the challenger. The proc uses the 26972 client record's hit mask, chance and duration. The handler requires the exact arena Kruul and the incoming damage to belong to the same phase and attempt. A fully absorbed hit, an unrelated attacker, a heal outside the fight or a late event after completion cannot heal Kruul.

The first cast is scheduled 18 seconds into combat and repeats every 45 seconds. These are reconstructed values. The 2018 Protection Paladin recording showed the stage transition near 3:14, Annihilate near 3:30 and the Twisted Reflection warning near 3:32. No second warning was visible in that short kill. The edited Protection Warrior guide shows the phase-one fight, the transition, the use of Spell Reflection or an interrupt, and the turn-in. Both recordings were sampled across their full lengths, with one-second frames through the Paladin phase-two transition and combat. The 2018 DBM module recognizes `234676` and announces an interrupt without providing a recurrence timer. [2018 Protection Warrior footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eFRnUCvaw3U), [2018 Protection Paladin footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4P6ZAZ6sQw), [original Protection Warrior guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/protection-warrior-mage-tower-challenge).

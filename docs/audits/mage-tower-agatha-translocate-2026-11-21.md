# Agatha's arena transitions

The existing Agatha controller had no arena relocation. The 26972 client has two two-second Translocate spells, `242987` and `242989`, with the same native nearby-destination teleport effect. The original DBM module recognizes the first spell as the transition. I sampled both the 520-second 2017 kill and the 505-second 2018 guide every six seconds, and reviewed the original kill's relocation portions at two-second intervals. The first cast moves Agatha to the raised end of the tunnel, and a later cast returns her to the lower end. [Original 2017 Fury kill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4a2esU-LCk), [2018 Fury guide footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cei8mN5Rrys), [July 2018 DBM module](https://github.com/DeadlyBossMods/DBM-Challenges/blob/cf9f66ea21818a6f503644599c5c4e1b28990a00/DBM-Challenges/LegionArtifacts/DPSImpossibleFoe.lua).

Only Agatha spawn `14534028` in the active map `1616`, scenario `1221` encounter uses the new rotation. She starts stationary, uses the first native cast at 50%, returns with the second spell after 35% or the repeat interval, and alternates sides every 68 seconds thereafter. Her controller leaves ongoing casts in place and advances the phase after the native spell has placed her at its configured destination. A rejected or interrupted cast keeps the previous phase and can retry. The existing imp death reaction and native victory remain in use.

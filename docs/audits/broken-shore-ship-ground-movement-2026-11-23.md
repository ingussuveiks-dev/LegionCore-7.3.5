# Broken Shore command ship combat movement

During Pillar of Fire the user reported that command ship NPCs appeared to swim instead of running. The screenshot identified Eredar Pyromancer. The five ship templates each allow ground movement, swimming and flight (`Ground=1, Swim=1, Flight=2`). All 15 relevant map 1666 spawns inherited those settings. The native creature update can mark these actors as flying when their height differs from the sampled ground by more than 0.05 yards. Movement splines inherit the flying flag, and flight and swim capabilities also admit paths that a ground actor would reject. The client report does not distinguish its flight pose from a swimming pose.

The installed map 1666 tile `16663426.mmtile` contains `NAV_GROUND` detail triangles directly beneath or above the coordinate of every selected actor. The closest same-XY ground mesh differs from each spawn height by at most 1.46 yards. The two recorded patrol paths use ordinary walk waypoints across the deck and ramp. The seven original combat or retreat spell records were also read from build 26972. Their known effects include attacks, auras and area creation. The boss uses its existing script to retreat.

Migration `2026_11_23_05` disables swim and flight for the exact 15 ship spawns. Their existing ground policy and all waypoint, position, combat and progression records stay intact. The spawn-specific change preserves explicit movement overrides and limits the fix to the confirmed decks. The map's airborne patrols elsewhere retain their original configuration.

MariaDB `EXPLAIN` resolved the expected 15 rows. The live 26972 run that reported the pose later completed scenario exit movie `637`, returned to Deliverance Point, and has the rewarded quest `46734` recorded. Those events predate this movement repair. No new DBErrors or crash reports were found. The data is queued for the next user-owned restart. Deck running, patrols and chasing on both levels need the in-game retest.

After shutdown, MariaDB readback confirmed `Swim=0, Flight=0` for all 15 actors. The migration and its updater receipt `36D0952563C6374F2BBCFBF8F632AD4E9A8DE663` committed together. The prior shutdown also reported eight map `1666` VMAP tile lifetime errors. Those belong to collision resource ownership and are independent of the movement data.

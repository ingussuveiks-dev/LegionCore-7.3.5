# Bile Spitter Eggs in Feltotem's Fall

The Legion 26972 WDB already contained Bile Spitter Egg `121459` and Bile Spitter `121460`. The world creature templates and the encounter's casting links were absent. The repaired Tugar AI releases two native egg missiles when its actual Earthquake aura expires after an uninterrupted cast. A control loss during the aura prevents release. The already-started stalactites and Jormog burrow continue through the interruption.

The native fixed-delay missile `242384` lands after one second and invokes `242378` using summon properties `61`. A live egg from this attempt starts the original 12-second cast `242379`. Its native periodic callback releases `243167` with properties `4059`. The script waits for actual spitter publication before consuming the egg, retries a failed summon at most three times and preserves the egg's remaining health fraction on the spitter. Destroyed eggs and stale attempts cannot hatch. Spitters use their normal threat and melee path and cast the native `242468` warning, `242566` missile and `242472` three-yard impact with its periodic Nature damage. The boss kill and scenario credit still use the existing SmartAI paths.

The June 2018 Beast Mastery recording was reviewed visually from 0:00 to 9:07 at five-second intervals, with one-second samples through 1:20–1:51 and 3:23–3:49. The first and later unbroken Earthquakes show two eggs near rock sites around six seconds after the warning. The January 2018 Destruction footage and its author's account describe interrupting Tugar to avoid eggs while the rocks remain. The soundtrack was not reviewed. Coordinates, spitter cast cadence and the health fraction transfer were reconstructed from the observed behavior and native data; exact per-specialization egg counts, other possible egg waves and spit timing remain client checks. [Beast Mastery recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yklXJT8SQ8E), [Destruction recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PeV7-YdbD1M), [Legion Discipline guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/discipline-priest-mage-tower-challenge).

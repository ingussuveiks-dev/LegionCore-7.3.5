# Prophet Velen's Holy Ward

Velen's Holy Ward uses the native eight-second cast `233473` and the existing two-and-a-half-yard area trigger `9586`. The ward accepts contact from a living friendly player and retains one charge. Its native `240521` heal still reaches friendly targets and disorients enemies for five seconds. A native heal hit on the challenger also removes their Twisted Reflection from the exact arena Kruul. A ward used while Twisted Reflection is reflected on Kruul leaves that hostile aura in place.

The previous Smite row interrupted every active cast, including Velen's own ward. Smite now waits for an active cast and the ward retains its priority. The ward timer begins during Velen's actual combat in either boss phase, with its first cast scheduled at eight to sixteen seconds and repeats at 33 seconds. This model follows the original DBM timer and does not recover the exact server scheduling or phase transition behavior of the original. The original Protection Warrior and Paladin recordings show the ward during combat and players using it as an encounter resource. A 2017 Protection Paladin account identifies the ward as a way to clear an unsuccessful Twisted Reflection interrupt. [2018 Protection Warrior footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eFRnUCvaw3U), [2018 Protection Paladin footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4P6ZAZ6sQw), [2017 Holy Ward account](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/2308163-How-I-beat-the-prot-paladin-mage-tower-guide).

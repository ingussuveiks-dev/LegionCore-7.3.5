# Lifespring Cavern — 2026-10-10

Scope: 39661 Lifespring Cavern, 39488 Balance of Elements, 39489 Invading Spelunkers, 39487 Crystal Fury, 39498 High Water. Native evidence and source hashes are in `highmountain-cavern-native-2026-10-10.json`.

## Repairs

* Restored objective 279636 Flags2=1 from TDB735. The item remains 128393, quantity ten, storage slot one. Both existing quest-only creature drop sources (95866 and 95916) remain unchanged, including their valid LootMode=0 semantics.
* Restored Violent Crageater 95916's native KillCredit1=95866. The original quest description explicitly allows both Drogbar Manathirsters and these earth furies; previously only the drogbar counted. Existing transformed Gelmogg 95882's credit alias 95881 is preserved.
* Balance of Elements and Invading Spelunkers are now parallel offers after Lifespring Cavern. A negative prerequisite group requires both rewards before Crystal Fury. Removed the old 39277 NextQuestID shortcut, which the core interprets as an alternative prerequisite and which would otherwise bypass this group. Native client RewardNextQuest fields are unchanged.
* Added the missing quest-starter relations for Jale 96520: 39489, 39487 and 39498. Existing stationary Rivermane Shaman 96038 relations are retained. The two legitimate delivery quests 39661 and 39498 have no native objectives: completing their log status on acceptance is correct, and reward still requires the destination NPC. Their actual enders 96520 and 97662 exist, with the needed Highmountain phase definitions. The downstream 39498 → 42104 prerequisite is preserved.
* Gelmogg's phase transition formerly required the current health to be between 40 and 50 percent. A larger nonlethal hit could skip it. The once-only trigger now covers 0–50 percent. A lethal kill still completes the ordinary kill objective; this change does not impose an invulnerable cinematic or alter shared damage handling.
* The transformation changes Gelmogg from entry 95881 to 95882 at twelve seconds, but Dargrul's signal at 15.5 seconds searched for 95881. Corrected that target to the actual transformed entry, allowing the existing second-phase spells to start. The original AI remains attached through `Creature::UpdateEntry`; its data-event receiver is retained. Native petrification 195597 lasts sixteen seconds. Dialogue, morph, spell IDs and valid 98–110 scaling remain intact.
* Connected Siphoning Crystal template 243639 to its existing quest-only loot table 243639. **There are no crystal object spawns in the live database**, and no exact placements were found in the local TDB735, original LegionCore database or current TDB archive. No coordinates were fabricated. Balance of Elements is executable through its two legitimate enemy drop sources; ground collection remains unavailable until reliable placements are obtained.

Behavior references: [contemporary Gelmogg playthrough](https://warcraft.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/crystal-fury-highmountain-quests-alliance), [Invading Spelunkers quest text/playthrough](https://warcraft.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/invading-spelunkers-highmountain-quests-alliance), and [Legion-era route showing parallel cavern tasks followed by Crystal Fury](https://www.wow-pro.com/highmountain-neutral/). Native QuestLine display order was not used as prerequisite proof.

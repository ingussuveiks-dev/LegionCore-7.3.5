# Fel Rupture in Feltotem's Fall

Tugar Bloodtotem `117230` now casts the Legion warning spell `241664` during the active map `1702`, scenario `1266` attempt. The first cast is scheduled at 7.5 seconds and the next at 10.9–13.4 seconds, matching the original DBM observations. The script snapshots the real player's ground position, publishes two crossing warning areas, and releases the matching impact after the 2.5-second time in the Legion spell data. A dead or absent player, a changed participant, a reset, or Tugar's death cancels the pending event.

The variants retain the native spell and trigger pairs `241665/241667` and `241668/241669`. Damage and knockback come from one native `241666` cast per eligible unit intersecting either arm. The center counts once. The damage pass snapshots the player's controlled unit list before casting, so a pet dying during the impact cannot invalidate iteration. An impact is allowed only after the actual warning and impact objects have both been published. Ground height, same-instance and same-phase checks scope the cast.

Existing shapes for related spell `217463` use 15 by 2.5 by 5-yard boxes with the same Legion impact visuals. The area-trigger loader divides each dimension by two to obtain the distance from the center. Feltotem uses these dimensions and its own native warning and impact spells. Its SQL entries use the loader's custom-key store, so publishing native entry `7562` does not replace either pre-existing `217463` row. The initial disposable data used the ordinary entry store and was replaced before promotion after source inspection exposed the two separate lookups.

The 2018 Beast Mastery run was reviewed visually from 0:00 to 9:07 at five-second intervals and from 1:02 to 1:26 at one-second intervals for the initial warning and impact. The soundtrack was not reviewed. The footage shows a golden cross followed by green spikes. Cross placement and rotation and synchronization of the two arms were reconstructed. [2018 Beast Mastery recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yklXJT8SQ8E), [2018 DBM Feltotem timers](https://github.com/DeadlyBossMods/DBM-Challenges/blob/cf9f66ea21818a6f503644599c5c4e1b28990a00/DBM-Challenges/LegionArtifacts/DPSFelTotem.lua).

# Legion quest offer flags after acceptance

The client continued showing the Accept/Decline panel for Khadgar's `47034` after acceptance. The log and the persisted active quest both confirm acceptance. `47035` was also accepted. Both quests use ordinary NPC offer flags, have no source spell or accept-time gossip override, and originate at Khadgar `116302`.

The 7.3.5 decoder expects the final three bit-fields in `SMSG_QUEST_GIVER_QUEST_DETAILS` as `DisplayPopup`, `StartCheat`, `AutoLaunched`. The server serialized `AutoLaunched` first and `DisplayPopup` last. The ordinary quest-giver query passes `true` for `AutoLaunched` and `false` for `DisplayPopup`, so the client interpreted it as a popup. The repair changes those two bits to the expected order. It retains the single serialization and the accepted quest path's empty `SMSG_GOSSIP_COMPLETE` packet. [Legion packet decoder](https://github.com/TrinityCore/WowPacketParser/blob/50d58553/WowPacketParserModule.V7_0_3_22248/Parsers/QuestHandler.cs).

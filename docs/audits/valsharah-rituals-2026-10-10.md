# Val'sharah ritual, escort and vigil audit, 2026-10-10

Scope: The Emerald Queen (38377), Heart of the Nightmare (38675/41724) and Dark Side of the Moon (41708/41890). This repairs server execution; no full client playthrough occurred.

## Findings and changes

- **38377:** SmartAI 91109/5 credited 92742 before casting the scene. Native revised scene 1146/package 1518 (spell 197680) has 28.5 seconds of waits in SceneScriptText 13744. Its native completion spell 197487 supplies credit 92742 and a post-scene teleport to 2606.18/6725.01/105.21 on map 1220. Gossip now starts the same scene without early credit. The spell handler waits 30 seconds, then uses 197487 only while the player is alive, still on the quest and within 80 yards of the grove. Client completion/cancel callbacks cannot advance the timer. A skipped or missing callback still reaches the timed completion; a logout, death at completion or departure requires replaying the gossip. Recasts do not award duplicate objective credit after completion.
- **38377 prerequisites:** the live data has three alternative rewarded Return to the Grove conditions (38148, 38322, 38323). The available quest list/dialogue does not prove that all three must be required simultaneously. Their OR relationship and predecessor checks are preserved and regression-tested, not changed into an inferred AND. This is a conservative compatibility decision, not certification of every historical retail unlock rule.
- **38675/41724:** the old personal Tyrande gave owner credit immediately when the NPC reached waypoint 6, even if the owner had not followed. The replacement uses all six existing waypoints in order, preserves Ash'alah display 67955, and pauses when the owner falls more than 35 yards behind. The owner must reach within 20 yards of each waypoint before progression. Only the final actual arrival grants 103022. Existing quest acceptance still casts custom spell 305067; its summon is explicitly made personal. Logout, map departure, death or abandonment clean up the actor. Returning to the original Tyrande within 60 yards restarts an unfinished escort without abandoning the quest.
- **41708/41890:** the old shared event completed on any 104646 death notification, even if previous wave enemies survived; it then credited nearby players. The new personal event retains the existing approach point, prayer aura 130491, enemy entries/placement and the 1/2/1 wave composition. A later wave only starts after every GUID from the preceding wave actually dies. Despawn, evade, missing summons, owner death/departure/abandonment and timeout cancel cleanly without credit. Retrying the original gossip creates a new event; another player's event is independent.
- Vigil completion requires actual return movement and the owner within 15 yards. Only the owner receives 103022. Native spell 207140 has area credit for 103022/104739 plus a phase effect, so it is intentionally not recast on surrounding players. Normal `Player::SetQuestStatus` already schedules `PhaseMgr::NotifyConditionChanged` and quest-object visibility updates when the quest completes. The legitimate meet-Tyrande objective 104645 remains on the static NPC. Dialogue now references existing groups 0/1/2/6/7/8; the old completion list referenced missing groups 3/4/5.

The first-person reports and objective descriptions on [The Emerald Queen](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=38377/the-emerald-queen), [Heart of the Nightmare](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=41724/heart-of-the-nightmare), and [Dark Side of the Moon](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=41708/dark-side-of-the-moon) corroborate the ritual, following and defense actions. Exact spell, objective and scene evidence/hashes are in `valsharah-rituals-native-2026-10-10.json`. The custom escort spell is explicitly identified as an existing overlay, not native DB2 content.

# Earthquake and Fel Hardened Scales in Feltotem's Fall

The Legion 26972 encounter uses Tugar Bloodtotem `117230`, Jormog `117484`, map `1702` and scenario `1266`. Tugar's previous script did not cast Earthquake. Jormog began with nine stacks of `238471`, and the SmartAI action for Tugar's death removed the entire aura. In the original fight, the remaining stacks matter when Tugar dies.

Tugar now casts native Earthquake `237950` and starts the rock warning and impact sequence after the aura is accepted. The three physical sites publish native `238302` triggers using entry `9923`. Impact `238027` retains the normal damage effect and adds the player knockback described by the Legion tooltip. Jormog uses the original burrow display `75542` and the 30-second burrow timer. His charges snapshot the real participant's position when the original warning `130585` plays. The moving worm's actual position between updates determines contact. Each published rock breaks once and consumes one stack. The broken-rock visual `243674` expires after 1.5 seconds. The encounter cleans up rocks on reset or boss death and cancels the burrow if its Tugar attempt is no longer active. Scenario completion, killing-blow ownership, and the Fel Rage branch remain on their existing SmartAI actions.

Two different spells were named Earthquake in the same extracted build. `241346` explicitly summons Deeplocs and belongs to a separate encounter. The 26972 description of `237950` references `238027` directly. The associated transform `238703` needs world template `119900` even though its WDB record already existed. The native WDB record also contains an invisible fallback model. The scoped burrow selects the visible original model.

The original January 2018 Destruction run was reviewed visually from 0:00 to 8:04 at five-second intervals, with one-second samples from 1:09 to 1:53 through the first warning, three rock positions, the charges and resurfacing. The soundtrack was not reviewed. The 20.5-second opening and 60-second repeat also match the July 2018 DBM observations. Coordinates, collision tolerance, knockback strength, the charge telegraph interval and broken-rock visual duration were reconstructed. The original encounter's archived server script and client-side measurements were unavailable.

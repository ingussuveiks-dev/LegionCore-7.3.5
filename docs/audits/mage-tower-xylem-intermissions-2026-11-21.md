# Closing the Eye intermissions

The previous world data switched Xylem from Frost to Arcane at 60% health. The original Survival Hunter recording shows three Razor Ice attacks before the first Mirror Images intermission, and another intermission after Arcane that returns to Frost. I sampled the 519-second recording every five seconds and examined the two intermissions at closer intervals. The timings used here are 90 seconds of Frost and 60 seconds of Arcane. The original DBM module identifies the two phase announcement spells, Arcane Annihilation and Shadow Barrage in both combat phases. The mirrored actors occupy the 12 existing arena positions with one of the eight outer positions reserved for the real boss. [Original Survival recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pnSf1I32Zgk), [July 2018 DBM module](https://github.com/DeadlyBossMods/DBM-Challenges/blob/cf9f66ea21818a6f503644599c5c4e1b28990a00/DBM-Challenges/LegionArtifacts/DPSXylem.lua).

An intermission pauses the applicable SmartAI combat rotation. Xylem starts the original 40-second Arcane Annihilation `234728`. The spell remains active until the arena's player reaches Xylem or reveals him with their actual Hunter Flare. The attempt checks the active quest, scenario, position, phase and player ownership. A completed or otherwise lost cast ends the attempt through normal evade and provides no reveal or scenario credit. The resumed phase uses the original Frost or Arcane rotation and its announcement. Frost returns with Shadow Barrage. The native 10% surrender and Corrupting Shadows summon and death remain in SmartAI.

The existing mirror WDB record `115543` supplies model `73863`. An image casts the original interruptible three-second Prismatic Shock `231914` with its six-yard damage and knockback. A real death from the eligible player or controlled unit publishes the original ten-second Arcane Barrier of Replenishment `231982` at that image's location. The barrier shrinks, protects against the normal arena damage and applies the native Time Acceleration `231980` for its 15% healing per second and 30% time rate. Darkness uses the native `232006` period and `232007` damage and slow. Leaving the bubble removes protection, and leaving or resetting the attempt cleans up its own hazard, mirrors and auras.

## Verification and limits

- The build 26972 catalog check verified the cast and barrier durations, image visual, knockbacks, area entries, cooldown effect and both phase spells. The source-extracted AddressSanitizer checks passed the ordinary phase countdown, surrender threshold, actual cast state, pet owner, cross-map and cross-phase exclusions, shrinking barrier, proximity and Flare admission, and canceled attempts. The prior Draw Power regression passed again.
- Real VMAP and Detour/MMAP checks confirmed supporting floor and complete paths at all 12 original locations. Authored height was approximately 0.084 yards above the floor at each point. The exact SQL applied twice in the isolated MariaDB fixture, then was applied manually and read back in production. The readback preserved the initial Frost chain, native surrender, final death, the Flare row and the 12 original anchors.
- The staged and canonical executables reached readiness against the isolated schema and shut down cleanly. The SHA-256 of both executables was `3E2DE659A17059140A04F1E7F2406543D8CA4CBBE6A8AED6BE3295B6DE771E7A`. The final linker map includes the active intermission callbacks. No new crashes, DBErrors, warnings or assertions were found. The verified `build-extractors/bin/Release/fixed/2026-11-21-mage-tower-xylem-intermissions/` archive contains 39 evidence hashes and records 191,496,976 bytes of redundant outputs removed.

The timer lengths, shrinking bubble radius, reveal distance, image casting cadence and the reveal location selection are reconstructed. The surviving client tests are the cloaking and barrier visual, real interrupt/knockback, direct approach and Flare reveal, death and relog, and the full encounter on each of its five specializations. Corrupting Shadows' ground and Seed of Darkness still require their own checks.

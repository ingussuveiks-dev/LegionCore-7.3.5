# Closing the Eye Seed of Darkness

The former Corrupting Shadows script scheduled three casts by the closest player eight seconds after its attempt to mark the victim. In build 26972, Seed of Darkness `233248` marks the player for eight seconds. The matching Portal Visual `233165` summons Darkness Within `116840` with properties `64`, and Merge `232991` has a native 20% heal.

The original Seed schedule remains in SmartAI. The actual selected victim must be the active participant or a controlled unit belonging to that participant. In the latter case the native spell marks the player. A successful application starts the existing Feeding on the Darkness Within aura. The player aura's real expiry issues three native summon casts at the player with Corrupting Shadows recorded as the original caster. Removal by death, reset, wrong owner, disconnection or pending transfer provides no summon. Each published add follows its own Corrupting Shadows and can use the native Merge only on arrival in range and line of sight. The hit consumes the add. An early kill provides no heal, and attempt cleanup and a finite summon expiry remove outstanding adds. The authored final boss death and Xylem quest/objective and outro remain in place.

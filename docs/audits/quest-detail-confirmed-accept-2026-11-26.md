# NPC quest details after a confirmed acceptance

The client showed “Quest accepted: The Trial at the Temple” and tracked its first objective while the Monkey King's offer still displayed Accept and Decline. Khadgar had shown the same symptom. The earlier server change refreshes a quest-only greeting when another eligible offer remains. A chained offer following a reward can arrive directly through the reward handler without passing through that greeting. The packet sequence for the reported click was not captured.

The stock Legion UI sends the acceptance request on click. Its details panel responds to the next greeting, offer or finish event. Adding a quest to the log alone leaves those details visible. The installed client addon records the quest ID only on a manual NPC offer click. The matching QUEST_ACCEPTED event supplies the log index and ID; the addon closes the still-visible offer through the normal panel lifecycle. Hiding the detail panel clears the pending ID, including when a subsequent greeting or offer replaces it. Failed admission and unrelated quest events cannot close it. Other clients require installation of the addon for this behavior. [7.3.5 QuestFrame](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/7.3.5/FrameXML/QuestFrame.lua) · [7.3.5 quest acceptance event](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/7.3.5/AddOns/Blizzard_ObjectiveTracker/Blizzard_ObjectiveTracker.lua)

Both installed files matched the repository copies by SHA-256. No separate test or agent-owned startup was run during active gameplay. The next client launch and acceptance at a multi-offer NPC remain unverified.

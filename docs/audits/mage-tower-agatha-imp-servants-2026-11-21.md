# Agatha's Imp Servants

Imp Servants `115643` and `115719` retain their original first-phase waypoints and the original first cast. Once they reach that waypoint, they can resume Funnel Energy after an interruption. After a completed Translocate, each servant follows the same Agatha that summoned it and resumes the channel on arrival. A later Translocate breaks the old channel and causes another approach. Funnel Energy `229928` uses its original periodic heal and selects the living summoner in the same attempt. A player using a near teleport such as Blink keeps the encounter active while the Fel Lava target waits for the movement acknowledgement.

The follow distance of two yards, five-yard channel-start check after relocation, and cast retry interval of 1.5 seconds are implementation choices. Native range, line of sight, casting restrictions and interrupt lockout still apply. The original wave roster and spawn positions remain as found in the server data. The guide describes a phase-two spawn from the opposite side of the middle, which needs a separate comparison with original positions. The original Fury guide also describes a resumed heal after interrupt, and the Unholy guide describes pursuit after Translocate.

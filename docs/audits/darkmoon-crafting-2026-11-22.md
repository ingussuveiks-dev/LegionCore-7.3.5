# Darkmoon profession crafting (29506, 29510, 29515–29517)

The case data identifies the original trainers on Darkmoon Island. `29506` uses Sylannia `14844/2172`, `29510` and `29515` use Sayge `14822/2226`, `29516` uses Chronos `14833/2313`, and `29517` uses Rinling `14841/2140`. Their original profession and 75-point requirements remain. The archived Shattrath variants share the questgiver entry, so the offer and turn-in marker also require map `974`. The server checks the map and active world event `75` at hand-in.

The native catalog links Cocktail Shaker `72043` to fizz spell `101988`, Discarded Weapon `72018` to `101985`, Exotic Herbs `71971` and Prophetic Ink `71972` to `101863/101868`, Bit of Glass `72052` to `102221`, and Craftsman's Kit `71977` to `101874`. The original item charges, materials, and self-target creation effects run under the accepted attempt and event check. Vendors for all native recipe ingredients have real world placements, including Sylannia for Fizzy Faire Drink. The recipes resolve to the caster's own bag even when another player is selected. Native reward and abandonment take the appropriate produced quest items, including the reusable Prophetic Ink, while purchased general trade goods keep their normal inventory rules.

The two stock templates `209283` and `209287` retain 47 weapon placements and 55 glass placements, their original loot-group IDs `40247/40250`, real quest-only drops, and Opening `3365` through lock `1691`. Their quest marker, lock handling, and loot window depend on an active Faire, the accepted incomplete quest, and a remaining material requirement. Existing group and quest-item loot checks remain. The marker and opening stop when the remaining requirement is covered by raw plus crafted items. Unrelated chests continue through their existing logic.

The pinned counterfactual reproduced crafting outside the quest and abandoned output in all five cases. It also reproduced opening empty loot on both material sources without the quest. The repaired native run checked the five actual turn-ins, all 102 stock positions, real loot pickups, skill threshold, item ownership and effect targets, event expiry, missing reagents, full backpack, and abandonment and reacceptance for inscription. Retained native regressions covered both Samophlange attempts and Thelgen's independent chest. One initial harness run loaded player templates before item templates. A later assertion assumed the native self-cast would reject a selected player. The final harness loads metadata in startup order and verifies who actually receives the crafted result.
